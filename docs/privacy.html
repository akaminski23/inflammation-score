Inflammation Score

Privacy Policy

Effective February 10, 2026

Local-First Architecture. Your wellness data is stored exclusively on your device. We have zero access to your information. No servers, no cloud, no exceptions.

What We Believe

Inflammation Score was built on a simple principle: your wellness journey is yours alone. We designed the App from the ground up with a local-first architecture, meaning your data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, we cannot access it, and we cannot share it — because it never reaches us.

Data You Provide

When you use the App, you voluntarily enter daily wellness observations:

  • Sleep quality ratings
  • Stress level assessments
  • Diet quality ratings
  • Exercise activity ratings
  • Calculated wellness patterns derived from your inputs

This information is stored in a local database on your iPhone. It is never transmitted to any external server, analytics platform, advertising network, or third party of any kind.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, process, or store:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or fingerprints for tracking
  • Location data
  • Health records or medical data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data

PDF Wellness Reports

The App allows you to generate PDF wellness summaries. These documents are created entirely on your device. If you choose to share a report via email, messaging, or other channels, that action is initiated and controlled by you through your device's native sharing interface. We are not involved in, and have no visibility into, any sharing you initiate.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following services for subscription management only:

  • RevenueCat — processes subscription transactions. RevenueCat receives only purchase-related data required by Apple and has no access to your wellness data. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store — handles payment processing. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Neither service receives any of your wellness patterns, scores, or daily observations.

Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion — use "Clear All Data" in Settings to permanently erase all wellness entries and preferences.
  • App Removal — uninstalling the App automatically removes all stored data from your device.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

Children

Inflammation Score is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly facilitate the collection of information from children.

Policy Updates

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the effective date above. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
